=====================================================================
WORKNEXUS - EMPLOYEE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM | SUPPORT & PRODUCT GUIDE
=====================================================================

OVERVIEW
---------------------------------------------------------------------
WorkNexus is a web-based HR and employee management platform. Teams
use it to manage employee records, attendance, leave requests,
client meetings, salary hikes, reports, and notifications.
Users sign in at the login page with a username and password.
What each person can see depends on their role (see ROLES below).

TECH AT A GLANCE
- Web application: works in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
- Built on CodeIgniter 3 (PHP) with MySQL/MariaDB.
- Google Calendar + Google Meet integration for client meetings.
- Email notifications via SMTP.

=====================================================================
USER ROLES AND WHAT THEY CAN ACCESS
=====================================================================

ADMIN - Full access to everything:
Employees, Departments, User Management, Reports, Settings,
Attendance (manage all), Leave, Meetings, Salary & Compensation,
Audit Trail, User Logs, view/edit all data including salary.

HR - Almost everything except system administration:
Employees, Departments, Reports, Attendance (manage all), Leave,
Meetings, Salary & Compensation. NO user management, settings,
audit trail, or user logs. Can edit employee data but not salary.

MANAGER - Supervisory access:
Dashboard, Employees (view only), Reports, Attendance, Leave,
Meetings. No departments, users, settings, audit, or salary access.

EMPLOYEE - Self-service access:
Dashboard, My Attendance, My Leave requests, My Meetings,
My Compensation (own salary page only). Cannot manage other
employees or see anyone else's data.

If a user says they cannot find a menu item, it is almost always
because their role does not include that permission. They should
contact an Admin if they believe they need more access.

=====================================================================
MODULES AND HOW TO USE THEM
=====================================================================

1) DASHBOARD
The first page after login. Shows overview statistics and charts
about employees, attendance, leave, and meetings depending on role.

2) EMPLOYEES (Admin, HR; Managers can view)
Purpose: Maintain the company employee directory.
How to add an employee:
  1. Go to "Employees" in the left sidebar.
  2. Click the "Add Employee" button.
  3. Fill in personal details (name, email, phone), job details
     (department, designation, joining date) and upload a profile
     photo.
  4. Save. The employee now appears in the directory.
Other actions: View full profile, Edit details, Delete.
Deleting an employee is a SOFT DELETE - the record is archived,
not permanently erased.

3) DEPARTMENTS (Admin, HR)
Purpose: Organize employees into departments (e.g., Engineering,
Sales, HR).
Steps: Sidebar > "Departments" > Add / Edit / Delete. Each
department page shows its employees.

4) USER MANAGEMENT (Admin only)
Purpose: Create login accounts and assign roles.
Steps: Sidebar > "User Management" > Add User > choose role
(Admin/HR/Manager/Employee) > save. Use Edit to change a role or
reset a password. If someone forgets their password, an Admin
resets it here.

5) ATTENDANCE
Official shift: 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST (Asia/Kolkata).
Key rules every user should know:
  - GRACE PERIOD: You may clock in up to 11:15 AM without penalty.
    Logging in AFTER 11:15 AM marks the day as HALF-DAY.
  - CLOCK-OUT WINDOW: A valid final clock-out counts from 6:30 PM.
    Logging out before 6:30 PM only ends your web session - it is
    NOT recorded as your attendance clock-out.
  - AUTO-CLOSURE: If you never clock out, the record automatically
    closes at 7:00 PM and your worked hours are counted until 7 PM.
  - OVERTIME: Only real clock-outs after 7:00 PM generate overtime
    (actual logout time minus 7:00 PM). An auto-closed record never
    produces overtime.
How employees use it: Sidebar > "My Attendance" > filter by month
and year to see your records, hours worked, and status.
Admins/HR use "Manage Attendance" to view and correct records for
all employees.

6) LEAVE MANAGEMENT
Leave types available: Sick, Casual, Personal, Maternity,
Paternity, Paid, Unpaid, Other.
Urgency levels: Low, Medium, High, Critical.
How to request leave (Employee):
  1. Sidebar > "Leave Management" > "Request Leave".
  2. Choose leave type, urgency, from-date, to-date, total days,
     and add a reason.
  3. Submit. The request goes to HR/Admin for review.
  4. Track status under "My Leaves" (Pending / Approved / Rejected).
How to approve/reject (HR/Admin):
  1. Sidebar > "Leave Management" > Manage Leave.
  2. Open a pending request, review details, click Approve or
     Reject and add remarks.
  3. The employee is notified instantly and by email.

7) CLIENT MEETINGS
Purpose: Schedule client meetings with Google Calendar and Google
Meet integration, plus file attachments (minutes, presentations).
How to schedule a meeting (HR/Admin):
  1. Sidebar > "Client Meetings" > Add Meeting.
  2. Enter title, client, date/time, agenda, location/link.
  3. Attach supporting files if needed.
  4. On first use you may be redirected to Google to authorize
     calendar access (one-time OAuth consent).
  5. Save - the meeting is added to Google Calendar with a Meet
     link and invites are emailed to participants.
Employees see "My Meetings" - read-only list of meetings they
attend. Edit or delete from the meeting detail page.

8) SALARY & COMPENSATION (HIKES)
Purpose: Propose and review salary hikes backed by attendance
scoring.
Workflow:
  1. HR/Admin opens "Salary & Compensation".
  2. Propose a hike for an employee - the system computes an
     attendance-based score to support the proposal.
  3. Proposal sits in Pending until reviewed.
  4. Reviewer Approves or Rejects; everything is visible under
     History.
Employees see only "My Compensation" - their own current salary
and hike history. Nobody else's.

9) REPORTS (Admin, HR, Manager)
Sidebar > "Reports". Attendance reports, leave summaries, and
employee analytics. Every table supports export buttons:
Copy, Excel, PDF, and Print. Filter by date range before exporting.

10) NOTIFICATIONS
Bell icon in the top header shows an unread count badge.
New notification alerts pop up automatically; the bell polls for
updates every 15 seconds while the tab is open.
Click any notification to jump to the related page.
Use "Mark all read" to clear the badge, or open Notifications >
View All for the full history.
You receive notifications for: leave approval/rejection decisions,
meeting updates, and other activity relevant to you.

11) AUDIT TRAIL (Admin only)
Every create/update/delete across the system is logged with who
did it, when, and the before/after values. Use it to answer "who
changed this?" questions.

12) USER LOGS (Admin only)
Records every login and logout with timestamp, IP address, and
browser/device info. Useful for security reviews.

13) PROFILE
Top-right avatar > "My Profile": update your personal information
and profile picture. Change Password is also available here.

14) SETTINGS (Admin only)
Application-wide configuration.

=====================================================================
UI TIPS FOR ALL USERS
=====================================================================
- DARK/LIGHT THEME: Moon/sun icon in the top header. Your choice
  is remembered per browser.
- QUICK PAGE SEARCH: Press Ctrl+K (or click the header search box)
  and type a page name like "leave" to jump anywhere instantly.
- TABLES: All lists have search, sorting, pagination, and rows per
  page (10/25/50/100).
- FULLSCREEN: The expand icon in the header toggles fullscreen mode.
- LOGOUT: Top-right avatar > Logout. Remember logging out during
  work hours does NOT clock you out of attendance.

=====================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=====================================================================

Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?
A: Contact an Administrator. Admins can reset your password under
User Management.

Q: Why can't I see some menus my colleague has?
A: Menus depend on your role. Ask an Admin if your role needs
updating.

Q: I logged in after 11:15 AM. What happens?
A: That day is automatically marked Half-Day.

Q: I logged out at 5 PM but my attendance still shows active?
A: Logging out before 6:30 PM only ends your session, not your
attendance. Your attendance will auto-close at 7:00 PM with hours
counted till 7 PM.

Q: I forgot to clock out yesterday. Will I lose pay?
A: No. The record auto-closes at 7:00 PM and hours are counted
until 7 PM. Contact HR/Admin if the record looks wrong.

Q: How do I get overtime credit?
A: Clock out normally after 7:00 PM. Overtime = your actual logout
time minus 7:00 PM.

Q: Who approves my leave?
A: HR or Admin. You will get a notification and email once decided.

Q: Where is the Google Meet link for a meeting?
A: Open the meeting from Client Meetings (or My Meetings). The
Meet link and calendar entry are attached after scheduling.

Q: Why am I redirected to Google when creating my first meeting?
A: One-time Google OAuth consent so WorkNexus can add events to
Google Calendar. After approving once, it will not ask again.

Q: How do I export a report?
A: Open Reports, filter what you need, then use the Copy, Excel,
PDF, or Print buttons above the table.

Q: Can employees see each other's salaries?
A: No. Employees can only view their own compensation page.

Q: Something in my record was changed wrongly - how do we find out
who did it?
A: Admins can check the Audit Trail which stores before/after
values of every change.

=====================================================================
ESCALATION
=====================================================================
For anything the assistant cannot resolve (password resets, role
changes, attendance corrections, salary disputes), collect the
user's name, role, and a description, and escalate to the HR team
or a WorkNexus administrator.
